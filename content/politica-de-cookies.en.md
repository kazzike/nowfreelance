### 1. What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that is stored in your browser when you visit a web page. Its purpose is for the website to be able to remember your visit when you return to browse that page.

### 2. Cookies used by this website

**This website uses essential technical cookies and analytics cookies:**

#### Essential Technical Cookies
- Basic website functionality cookies
- Security and SPAM protection cookies (provided by Cloudflare)

#### Analytics Cookies (Cloudflare Analytics)
We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses cookies to collect anonymous information about:
- Number of visitors and page views
- Aggregated browsing behavior
- Site performance metrics

**Important:** Cloudflare Analytics does not use cross-site tracking cookies and data is collected anonymously.

### 3. How to manage cookies?

You can set your browser to reject all cookies, although this may affect the functioning of the site. Here is how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

* **Google Chrome:** [https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
* **Mozilla Firefox:** [https://support.mozilla.org/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences)
* **Microsoft Edge:** [https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/delete-and-manage-cookies-168dab11-0753-043d-7c16-ede5947fc64d](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/delete-and-manage-cookies-168dab11-0753-043d-7c16-ede5947fc64d)
* **Safari (Apple):** [https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac)
